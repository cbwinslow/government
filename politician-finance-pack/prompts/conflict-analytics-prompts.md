# Conflict of Interest / Analytics Prompts

## Prompt 1: Trade vs committee conflict scan
For all members of Congress serving on committees with regulatory purview over financial markets (Banking, Finance, Ways & Means, Commerce), cross-reference their personal stock transactions from financial disclosures against the industries and companies under their committee's jurisdiction. Flag any transaction in a company whose industry falls under the politician's committee purview. Generate a ranked list by total dollars traded.

## Prompt 2: Donor-issue alignment report
For {politician_name}, extract their top 10 campaign contributors by dollar amount from OpenSecrets. For each contributor's industry, find the politician's floor speeches and votes on related legislation. Use the Honesty Engine to determine if the politician's actions align with the contributor's interests or the public interest. Flag any contradictions.

## Prompt 3: Temporal trade analysis
For {politician_name}, analyze the timing of their stock trades relative to their committee activities and public statements. For each transaction: (1) get the transaction date, (2) look up committee hearings and meetings in the preceding 30 days that involved the company or its industry, (3) check floor speeches mentioning the company or sector. Report any trades that occurred within 7 days of a relevant committee action.

## Prompt 4: Sector concentration dashboard
Build a data model that aggregates all congressional stock transactions by sector (using ticker → sector mapping). For each sector, identify: (1) total dollars traded by members of Congress, (2) which committees have purview over that sector, (3) which politicians are both trading and serving on relevant committees, (4) the net buy/sell trend by sector over time. Create a materialized view for dashboard consumption.

## Prompt 5: PTR filing completeness audit
For the current year, audit all PTR (Periodic Transaction Report) filings. For each member of Congress who is required to file, check: (1) was a PTR filed for every qualifying transaction within 45 days? (2) are there gaps in filing history? (3) are there discrepancies between what was reported and what public information suggests? Generate an audit report with compliance rate percentage.

## Prompt 6: Lobbying overlap detection
For each major piece of legislation in the current Congress, identify: (1) the bill's industry impact (which sectors gain or lose), (2) the top lobbying spenders on that bill, (3) which members of Congress received campaign contributions from those lobbying entities, (4) how those members voted. Use the Honesty Engine to flag members who voted against their stated position and in favor of a donor's position.

## Prompt 7: Asset divestment monitor
For politicians who have announced divestment from certain sectors or assets, monitor their financial disclosures for: (1) continued holdings in those assets beyond the divestment date, (2) new transactions in companies they claimed they would avoid, (3) family member transactions in restricted assets. Generate alerts for any detected violations of their own divestment promises.

## Prompt 8: Corporate board cross-reference
Cross-reference politician financial disclosures against corporate board rosters for the S&P 500. For each politician: (1) identify corporate board memberships, advisory roles, or significant stock holdings, (2) compare against committee assignments and sponsored legislation, (3) flag any case where a politician's corporate position intersects with their legislative authority. Generate an influence network graph.

## Prompt 9: Insider trading heuristic scanner
Apply statistical heuristics to detect potential insider trading patterns in congressional stock trades: (1) abnormal returns within 30 days of a trade, (2) trades occurring before major legislation or regulatory actions, (3) trades in companies on the politician's committee jurisdiction list, (4) large trades (>$500K) vs the politician's disclosed net worth, (5) timing of trades relative to earnings reports and major public filings. Score each trade on a 1-10 suspicion scale.

## Prompt 10: Ethics compliance portal
Design a public-facing dashboard that shows: (1) real-time STOCK Act compliance status for each member of Congress (PTR filing timeliness, disclosure completeness), (2) historical trading activity with search by name, ticker, date range, and committee, (3) conflict-of-interest flags automatically generated by the cross-reference engine, (4) Honesty Engine scores for financial ethics, (5) download access to all source filings. Build the API endpoints and React components.
